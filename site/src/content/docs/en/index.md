Welcome to the Cloud Training course. It guides you through Docker fundamentals — organised as **Modules** with explanation and a **Lab**, each verifiable via a **Flag** produced by a local **Verifier** container.

The course has three sections:

- **Prologue** — Setup and basics before you start.
- **Core** — Five core modules in fixed order.
- **Advanced** — Optional modules, freely chosen after the core.

Pick your entry point from the navigation on the left.
